# Avantree AS50

`as50` is a page of the Pshell settings for the Avantree AS50 (`scripts/ipc.sh
as50 open`), the headphones of Avantree's Ensemble TV set, also sold alone and
usable as a plain Bluetooth headset. Made with the unit 02:8C:7D:00:32:5F;
every unit matches by its Bluetooth name. The plugin is off by default; the
headset need not be there – BlueZ's signals say when it comes and goes, and
what was picked here (codec, lip sync, default output) goes on again each
time it connects.

- `scripts/as50/as50.py` – the daemon (JSON lines on stdin and stdout), `dump`
  prints what BlueZ, PipeWire and SDP say. `PSHELL_AS50_DRY=1` writes
  nothing (logged) and leaves the buttons to the desktop.
- `scripts/as50/sdp.py` – SDP over L2CAP PSM 1 without root: every service
  record, also those BlueZ leaves out of its UUID list.
- `core/services/As50.qml` – starts it (kept alive by `shell.qml`), keeps
  `as50.json`, the volume OSD for changes made on the headset, the battery
  warning, WirePlumber's auto-switch setting.
- `core/views/settings/As50Page.qml`, `as50/As50Figure.qml` (the headset,
  live: cups as loud as its own volume, the buttons lighting up, the lamp, the
  battery, the microphone glowing). The action picker is the mouse's with
  `store: As50`.
- IPC: `as50 open`, `connect|disconnect|toggle`, `mic on|off|call|toggle`,
  `codec aac|sbc|sbc-xq|faststream|next`, `battery`, `state`.

## What it is (reverse engineered 2026-11-03)

Bluetooth Classic only (BR/EDR, no LE, no GATT), class 0x240404 (audio,
wearable headset). No vendor app exists for it and it has no vendor channel:
SDP lists exactly five records, no SPP, no Qualcomm GAIA, no Device ID.

| Record | Version | Where | Features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Headset (HSP) | 1.2 | RFCOMM 2 | remote volume |
| Audio Sink (A2DP) | 1.2 | PSM 25 | 0x01 headphone |
| AVRCP controller | 1.4 | PSM 23 | 0x01 category 1 |
| AVRCP target | 1.4 | PSM 23 | 0x02 category 2 (absolute volume) |
| Handsfree (HFP) | 1.5 | RFCOMM 3 | 0x1F: EC/NR, 3-way, CLI, voice recognition, remote volume |

A2DP endpoints: SBC (bitpool 2–53), AAC (MPEG-2/4 LC, 8–48 kHz, VBR up to
320 kb/s) and CSR FastStream (vendor 0x0A codec 0x01) with the back channel
(sink 48/44.1 kHz, source 16 kHz). PipeWire offers AAC, SBC, SBC-XQ,
FastStream, FastStream duplex and HFP; HFP is CVSD only (no codec negotiation
in its features: no mSBC).

What it says over HFP when it connects (btmon): `AT+BRSF=63`, `AT+CIND=?`,
`AT+CIND?`, `AT+CMER=3,0,0,1`, `AT+VGS=14`, `AT+BTRH?`, `AT+XAPL=0000-0000-0100,7`
(Apple accessory: battery, dock, Siri), `AT+IPHONEACCEV=2,1,9,2,0` (battery
level 9 → 100 %, dock state 0) and `AT+CSRSF=0,0,0,1,0,0,0` (CSR extensions,
asking for battery reports – PipeWire says ERROR, the Apple one is enough).
BlueZ gets the battery from PipeWire's battery provider (10 % steps).

### The buttons

| On the headset | Over the air | Here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ⏯ once | AVRCP PLAY / PAUSE (its own idea of the state) | KEY_PLAYCD / KEY_PAUSECD on "Avantree AS50 (AVRCP)" |
| + / − once | its own volume, AVRCP volume-changed (16 steps of 0–127) | MediaTransport1.Volume |
| + held | AVRCP FORWARD | KEY_NEXTSONG |
| − held | AVRCP BACKWARD | KEY_PREVIOUSSONG |
| ⏯ twice | HFP `AT+BLDN` (redial), PipeWire answers ERROR | only through WirePlumber's log |
| ⏯ + + held | nothing (the sound mode, see below) | – |
| ⏯ + − held | nothing | – |
| ⏯ held 5 s | pairing mode (lamp red/blue) | – |
| power switch | disconnects | Device1.Connected |

niri binds no media keys, so the daemon takes the AVRCP input device (grab)
and runs the shell's play / next / previous, or a rule per button and app.
*Buttons through the shell* off gives the keys back to the focused window.

⏯ twice can only be seen in PipeWire's HFP backend. *Hear ⏯ pressed twice*
sets the `log.level` of WirePlumber's clients in the `settings` metadata to
`<their level>,spa.bluez5.native:4` (only while the headset is connected),
follows `journalctl --user -u wireplumber` for `RFCOMM << …` and puts every
level back as it was when it stops. The log has no address: with two HFP
headsets connected a line may be the other's. It also gives the battery and
the dock state (`AT+IPHONEACCEV`) and the speaker gain (`AT+VGS`).

### Only on the headset

The sound mode – clear voice or balanced – switches with ⏯ and + held
together (the headset says "Balanced Mode"); it is kept over power off, a
reset brings clear voice back, batches before T24D2 lack it. Nothing goes
over Bluetooth for it (checked with btmon), so the page explains it and only
notes which one the user picked. Pairing with the Ensemble transmitter and
adding a second AS50 happen on the transmitter.

## Settings

- Codec for music (`options.music`): AAC (best, ~200 ms), SBC-XQ, SBC,
  FastStream (low latency). Set again on each connect while an A2DP profile
  is active.
- Microphone: off, *Mic + music* (FastStream duplex: stereo stays, mic 16 kHz)
  or *Call* (HFP CVSD, mono 8 kHz). The headset's noise suppression sends
  exact zeros while nobody speaks. WirePlumber's
  `bluetooth.autoswitch-to-headset-profile` (every headset) is a switch here.
- Volume: PipeWire's sink volume is the headset's own (AVRCP absolute volume);
  the headset keeps it across reconnects. Changes made on it show an OSD.
- Lip sync: the port latency offset (`pactl set-port-latency-offset`), set on
  each connect; the headset reports no delay itself.
- Connection: name (BlueZ alias), trusted, take it over every 30 s while it is
  on elsewhere, default output on connect, pause on disconnect (Media, for
  every pair of headphones), the battery warning (default 20 %).

## Tests

All with the daemon alone (not the live shell), each change put back:
codec AAC → FastStream → AAC, FastStream duplex (source and level stream),
AVRCP volume 34 → 40 → 34, latency 0 → 120 → 0 ms, the log tap on and off
(the metadata compared before and after), disconnect and connect (AAC, the
volume, the battery and the buttons come back). Offscreen page shots:
`~/.cache/pshell-worktrees/as50-tools/preview.sh <worktree> <outdir>`
(`DRY=0` lets the daemon write).

Not tested on the real thing: button presses through the grab (the presses
and their codes are from the capture), ⏯ twice through the log tap, the
microphone with someone speaking, the battery warning, take-over from the
Ensemble transmitter.
